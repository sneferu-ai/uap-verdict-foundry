# Verdict Foundry — Production Brand Identity

The Phase 2 production brief explicitly replaced the prototype's cropped
raster mark. The original B13 direction board and exact raster are retained as
immutable provenance; the shipped identity is the original, code-native vector
system in `BRAND_ASSETS/`. Read `BRAND_DIRECTION.md` before extending it.

- Direction provenance: `BRAND_ASSETS/brand-direction.png`
- Historical frozen raster: `BRAND_ASSETS/brand-mark.png`
- Production primary mark: `BRAND_ASSETS/brand-mark.svg`
- Production horizontal lockup: `BRAND_ASSETS/brand-lockup.svg`
- Monochrome/print mark: `BRAND_ASSETS/brand-mark-monochrome.svg`
- App icon: `ui/public/app-icon.svg`
- Product-surface copy: `ui/public/brand-mark.svg`

Two evidence paths converge through a neutral aperture and leave as one
resolved record. The ledger line grounds the form in documentation; it must
never be restyled as a spacecraft, star, or sensational UFO motif. The primary
mark's self-contained charcoal field works on both light and dark surfaces;
use the monochrome asset for one-colour print, embossing, and stamps.

## Required semantic color tokens

- `--brand-background`: `#1C1E25`
- `--brand-foreground`: `#ABDFB9`
- `--brand-muted`: `#7A9D87`
- `--brand-primary`: `#ABDFB9`
- `--brand-secondary`: `#707172`
- `--brand-surface`: `#323539`

Apply semantic colours through the product token layer. Never use quarantine
burgundy as a forensic success colour. Preserve accessible contrast, the SVG
title/description, and clear space equal to one aperture radius.
