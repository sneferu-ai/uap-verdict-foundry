# SOUL.md — UAP Verdict Foundry Operator Console

## Who is using this and what hurts?

Elias runs the Foundry as a one-person analysis bureau from a converted
study in Tucson: buyers email him capture files, he submits them, and the
pipeline returns verdicts overnight. He is at this console at 6:40am with a
second coffee, Terminal open on the left monitor (`uapvf audit verify` is
his religion), this console on the right, fourteen cases queued from a busy
weekend and at least one of them failed at the quality gate. What hurts is
not volume — it is *tone*. His entire business rests on the B13 law that no
output of this system ever claims extraterrestrial origin; a dashboard that
sensationalizes a `no_mundane_match` with glow or exclamation marks would be
a legal and epistemic liability he'd burn to the ground. He also just paid
real money per case (the spend cap is law too), so a UI that hides cost,
lies about a button's effect, or buries a failed stage under polite copy
costs him trust he cannot buy back. Density over breath. Honesty over
politeness. Flat facts at 6:40am.

## What does this product feel like to use, hour 14 of 14?

It feels like **Linear** at 2am — the issue console, not the marketing
site. A dark, quiet, charcoal surface where every reading is exact and
nothing performs excitement: rows of cases hang with poster-like hierarchy,
one oversized signal per case (its verdict, its status), everything else
sparse supporting detail. Every action has a visible, audible-in-its-
precision consequence — a retry says what it will cost before it runs, a
delete names exactly what will be destroyed, a fiction seed stays visibly
quarantined behind its label. You can stay in it for fourteen hours because
it never makes you wait, never guesses, and never raises its voice.

## What does this explicitly NOT feel like?

This console is four things it will never be. Not X-Files merchandise: no
glowing-green alien kitsch, no radar-sweep animations, no "UNIDENTIFIED!"
exclamations, no sci-fi HUD neon — a `no_mundane_match` verdict is a
procedural outcome and wears the same visual weight as any other row. Not a
Vercel-style SaaS dashboard with a gradient hero and three evenly-spaced KPI
stat cards across the top; spend is one honest bar, not a hero band. Not a
generic admin template — no Inter font, no slate-50 background, no
indigo-600 buttons, no evenly-spaced white cards with soft drop shadows; the
Foundry is dark charcoal (`#1C1E25`) with sage green (`#ABDFB9`), flat
graphic planes, hairline rules, and a characterful display face. Not a
tabloid: no "Oops!", no "We couldn't find what you were looking for 🛸", no
celebratory microcopy, no consumer-cute voice mixed with product-terse — one
voice, precise, direct, human.
