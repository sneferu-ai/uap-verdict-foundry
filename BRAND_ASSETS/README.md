# Verdict Foundry identity assets

The production identity replaces the provisional cropped raster mark from the
prototype. Two differently coloured evidence paths converge through a neutral
aperture into one resolved path. The small ledger line/dot anchors the mark in
documentary evidence rather than sensational imagery.

- `brand-mark.svg`: primary dark-background mark; safe from 16 px to print.
- `brand-mark-monochrome.svg`: single-colour print, emboss, receipt, and stamp use.
- `brand-lockup.svg`: horizontal editorial lockup for title cards and delivery.
- `brand-direction.png`: retained as the original visual-direction board.
- `brand-mark.png`: retained only as the historical prototype raster.

The primary SVG includes a title and description. Do not remove its clear
space, recolour evidence paths to red, add stars/saucers, or use the speculative
burgundy quarantine colour for forensic status.
